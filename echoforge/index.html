The Problem

Content creation should not require a recording studio

Independent creators and small teams lose time, money, and reach because audio production remains locked behind expensive equipment and steep learning curves.

The equipment barrier

A decent podcast setup costs $500 to $2,000 for microphones, audio interfaces, acoustic treatment, and recording software. Most creators never start because of this upfront cost.

72% of creators cite equipment cost as the top barrier to starting a podcast

The editing nightmare

Editing a 30-minute podcast episode takes 2 to 4 hours of post-production work. Removing ums, adjusting levels, cutting silence, and mastering audio drains time that could go into creating.

Content creators spend 3.2 hours editing for every 1 hour of published audio

The reach gap

Written content is consumed passively. Audio reaches audiences during commutes, workouts, and chores. Without a podcast version, you miss 63% of potential audience who prefer listening.

Podcast listeners consume 2.8x more content per week than text-only readers
