The Problem

AI agents lack boundaries

Without guardrails, autonomous AI systems make expensive, hard-to-reverse decisions.

Uncontrolled Spending

AI agents managing inventory or procurement often order in bulk without approval. A Stockholm cafe saw its AI order 3x the normal weekly supply of ingredients, racking up $4,200 in unsold waste in one month.

AI-run businesses report 2.7x higher supply wastage than human-managed equivalents

Hidden Decision Drift

AI agents gradually shift behavior as their training context changes. Without oversight, an agent that was conservative with pricing one month can become aggressive the next, undercutting margins without human awareness.

Behavior drift detected in 63% of long-running AI agents within 90 days

No Audit Trail

When an AI agent makes a bad call, owners have no record of why. A cafe manager could not determine why its AI ordered 200kg of avocados in a single day, with no logs or constraint records to review.

87% of SMBs using AI agents have no decision audit capability in place
Features

Guardrails that work out of the box

Three layers of protection designed for non-technical business owners.

01

Budget & Resource Caps

Set hard limits on spending, inventory quantities, and resource allocation that your AI agents simply cannot exceed. Configure daily, weekly, or per-decision caps with automatic notification when approaching thresholds.

In practice: A cafe owner sets a $200 daily ingredient cap. When the AI tries to order $350 of produce, BoundAI blocks the order and sends an alert. The owner reviews and approves — or rejects.
02

Behavioral Guardrails

Define boundaries for pricing changes, inventory decisions, and customer interactions. Guardrails run continuously, flagging when agent behavior shifts outside acceptable parameters before decisions are executed.

In practice: A retail AI's pricing guardrail blocks a 40% discount on premium items. The owner sees the attempted deviation in the dashboard and can adjust the guardrail for a seasonal sale — intentionally, not accidentally.
03

Decision Audit Log

Every AI decision — approved, blocked, or flagged — is recorded with full context: timestamp, trigger, guardrail invoked, and outcome. Searchable, exportable, and understandable without technical training.

In practice: End-of-month review shows the AI attempted 14 blocked decisions and passed 86. An owner spots a pattern: the AI keeps trying to order from an expensive supplier. The guardrail is tuned, saving $600/month.
