Features

Built for teams that need to move fast without breaking things

DepShield runs in the background, watches your lock files, and only interrupts you when something actually matters.

01

Real-time Threat Detection

DepShield continuously monitors your package-lock.json, yarn.lock, and requirements.txt against a live threat feed that updates within minutes of new attack disclosures.

In practice: When the Mini Shai-Hulud campaign adds a new compromised npm package, DepShield alerts you within ~4 minutes — not days later when a CVE is published.
02

Automated Patch Resolution

When DepShield detects a compromised package, it automatically suggests and tests safe replacements or version bumps — no manual research needed.

In practice: DepShield detects uuid@8.3.2 has been compromised. It auto-suggests uuid@8.3.3 (verified), runs your test suite, and opens a PR. All in 3 minutes.
03

Dependency Graph Visualizer

See every direct and transitive dependency in a clean interactive graph. Understand exactly which packages introduce risk and which ones are safe.

In practice: A developer discovers left-pad@1.3.0 is pulled in by 3 different packages in their tree. DepShield shows the chain and suggests consolidating to a single pinned version — reducing attack surface by 67%.
