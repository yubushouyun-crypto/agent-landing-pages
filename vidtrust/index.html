Video KYC is under siege

As banks and fintechs push video-based identity verification, AI-generated fraud is growing faster than most detection tools can keep up.

◆

Deepfake identity theft

AI-generated face swaps and deepfake videos can now pass traditional KYC checks. Attackers use real-time face filters to impersonate customers during live video calls.

Banks reported a 72% increase in synthetic identity fraud attempts using AI-generated media in 2025

Pre-recorded playback attacks

Fraudsters replay stolen video recordings during KYC sessions. Standard motion-detection checks -- blink, nod, smile -- are easily bypassed with recorded responses.
