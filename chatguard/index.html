Every chatbot interaction is logged with full context: the prompt, the AI's response, the action taken (or blocked), and the verification steps attempted. Tamper-proof audit logs for compliance and forensic investigation.

Policy Enforcement Engine

Define granular policies for what your AI chatbot is allowed to do. Block high-risk operations like email changes, password resets, and payment modifications. Require step-up verification for sensitive actions.
