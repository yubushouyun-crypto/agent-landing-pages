**Narrative:** A SaaS company discovers that Claude has been telling potential customers their pricing starts at $49/mo when they raised it to $79/mo six months ago. ## Core Features

### Feature 1: AI Search Monitor

Real-time tracking of brand appearance across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity. Covers product descriptions, pricing mentions, competitive comparisons, and overall sentiment.

**Detailed Explainer:** The system sends structured queries to each AI platform daily, collecting every mention, description, and recommendation related to the brand. Results are normalized and categorized by sentiment, accuracy, and competitive positioning. The dashboard shows trends over time, share of voice vs. competitors, and AI-specific metrics not available in any other tool.

**In Practice:** A DTC brand discovers ChatGPT consistently recommends a competitor over them for 40% of product queries. Within a week, they identify the gap in their AI training data and close it.

### Feature 2: Sentiment and Accuracy Audit

Real-time hallucination detection that alerts brands when AI models present incorrect pricing, outdated features, or fabricated claims. Includes a severity scoring system.

**Detailed Explainer:** The audit engine cross-references AI model outputs against the brand's verified data sources (pricing page, product specs, official documentation). Any discrepancy generates an alert with severity level (critical, major, minor). Historical audit logs enable trend analysis and team accountability.

**In Practice:** A SaaS company is alerted that Claude is describing their pricing as "starting at $49/mo" when they raised prices to $79/mo six months ago. They update their structured data and the hallucination resolves in 48 hours.

### Feature 3: AI Optimization Score

An actionable score (0-100) measuring AI brand health across three dimensions: visibility (how often the brand appears in AI responses), accuracy (how correctly AI describes the brand), and sentiment (positive vs. negative framing).

**Detailed Explainer:** The optimization engine analyzes what content and signals influence AI model outputs. It provides a prioritized action plan with specific recommendations: update structured data, publish certain types of content, improve FAQ pages, add schema markup, or adjust product descriptions. Each recommendation includes an estimated impact on the overall score.

**In Practice:** An e-commerce brand improves from a score of 42 to 89 by implementing structured data, publishing authoritative product content, and adding AI-friendly FAQ pages. AI-referred traffic increases 3x.
