## User Pain Points

### Pain Point 1: Hidden downtime bleeds production capacity

Small manufacturers have no real-time visibility into machine performance. Issues develop gradually — vibration drift, temperature creep, pressure loss — and go unnoticed until they cause catastrophic failure. Each unplanned event means hours or days of lost production.

- **Supporting data**: Unplanned downtime costs manufacturers $50B annually. Small factories lose 5-15% of capacity to undetected issues.
- **Concrete narrative**: A Midwest injection molding shop with 12 presses had a 20-year-old press that would seize up every 3-4 months. Each failure cost $8,000 in repairs and 2 days of lost production. They had no way to predict when it would happen next.

### Pain Point 2: Critical knowledge walks out the door when senior operators leave

Over 75% of manufacturing process knowledge exists only in the heads of experienced operators. When they retire or leave, their expertise — setup procedures, troubleshooting steps, quality telltales — disappears with them. New operators take months to reach full productivity.

- **Supporting data**: 75% of manufacturing knowledge is undocumented tribal knowledge. Average time to train a new operator to full proficiency is 6-12 months.
- **Concrete narrative**: A 40-year veteran toolmaker at a precision job shop announced his retirement. The company realized 87 unique machine setups existed only in his notebook and memory. Training his replacement would take 9 months.

### Pain Point 3: Spreadsheets and paper logs can't provide real-time insights

Most small manufacturers track production, quality, and maintenance in disconnected spreadsheets, paper logbooks, or outdated ERP modules. No real-time dashboard, no cross-machine trend analysis, no ability to detect subtle patterns that precede failures.

- **Supporting data**: 82% of small manufacturers rely on spreadsheets for production operations tracking (industry survey).
- **Concrete narrative**: A packaging line had a recurring misfeed issue every 3-4 days. The maintenance team logged each incident in a paper notebook. It took 3 months and 15 hours per month of troubleshooting before someone noticed the misfeeds always followed a pressure drop pattern.

## Core Features

### Feature 1: Real-time Line Monitoring

Wireless sensors connect to existing equipment (built 1990+) with no API integration required. PlantIQ's AI learns each machine's normal operating parameters within 72 hours. The system continuously monitors vibration, temperature, pressure, cycle times, and energy consumption. Anomalies are flagged in real-time with severity ratings and recommended actions.

**In practice**: A small injection molding shop connected 3 sensors to their oldest press. Within a week, PlantIQ detected a vibration pattern that preceded bearing failure by 48 hours — saving $12,000 in repairs and 3 days of lost production.

### Feature 2: Voice-to-SOP Knowledge Capture

Experienced operators use PlantIQ's mobile app to speak their workflows naturally. The AI converts spoken instructions into structured, searchable standard operating procedures with step descriptions, timing estimates, tool requirements, and quality checkpoints. Each SOP is indexed by machine type, process category, and operator.

**In practice**: A 40-year veteran toolmaker at a Midwest job shop recorded 87 setup procedures in one afternoon using PlantIQ's voice capture. New operators now find answers in seconds via search instead of spending months shadowing senior staff.

### Feature 3: Predictive Maintenance Alerts

Machine learning models analyze sensor data, maintenance history, and production patterns to forecast failures days in advance. Alerts include estimated time to failure, likely root cause, recommended parts and tools, and suggested repair window for minimal production impact.

**In practice**: A packaging line was throwing random misfeeds every 3-4 days. PlantIQ correlated the pattern with pneumatic pressure drift and scheduled a $200 valve replacement during planned downtime — eliminating 15 hours of monthly troubleshooting.

## Use Case Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Precision Machining Shop
A 15-employee CNC shop with 8 machines. Spindle failures cause $5,000+ repairs and 2-day downtime each. PlantIQ monitors spindle vibration and temperature on each machine. When vibration drift reaches 70% of the failure threshold, PlantIQ alerts the maintenance lead to schedule bearing inspection during the next tool change window.

### Scenario 2: Injection Molding Facility
A 50-employee molding facility with 22 presses. Barrel temperature instability causes flash defects on a critical medical device mold. PlantIQ detects a 3-degree drift pattern over 8 hours and alerts the production manager. The issue is traced to a failing heater band and replaced during a scheduled mold change — preventing $30,000 in potential scrap.

### Scenario 3: Food Packaging Line
A 30-employee packaging operation. Conveyor speed drift causes inconsistent fill weights on a high-volume snack line. PlantIQ's monitoring detects the drift pattern and alerts the line lead, who recalibrates the drive motor. The issue is corrected in 10 minutes instead of the usual 3 hours of trial-and-error troubleshooting.

### Scenario 4: Electronics Assembly
A 25-employee electronics contract manufacturer. The sole SMT technician with 20 years of experience plans to retire. PlantIQ's voice-to-SOP tool captures 45 reflow profile setup procedures, 12 pick-and-place changeover sequences, and 30 troubleshooting workflows in a single week — preserving institutional knowledge.
