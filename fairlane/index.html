The Problem

Platforms Are Losing Their Workforce

Mass strikes, regulatory pressure, and algorithmic distrust are threatening the gig economy model. Most platforms have no visibility into the root causes.

Mass Worker Unrest

In December 2025, over 50,000 gig workers in Hyderabad alone went on strike, demanding an end to 10-minute delivery windows and fairer pay. Platforms had no data-driven way to predict or prevent the walkout.

50,000 workers striking across 1 city

Algorithmic Distrust

Workers cite unfair dispatch algorithms, opaque rating systems, and disappearing incentives as top grievances. Without algorithmic auditing, platforms cannot prove fairness or identify the patterns driving churn.

Regulatory Exposure

Politicians are calling for PM intervention on gig worker rights. France is banning under-15s from social media. Labor regulators globally are circling. Platforms need compliance tooling before the laws arrive.
